Small House Builder
Design a small, code-built cabin or studio you can actually build — and price.
  1. Tune the build. Set size, roof, foundation (stilts or slab) and form with the controls on the left — the 3D model updates live.
  2. Or just describe it. Tell the Architect what you want — “a 10×12 insulated studio, gable roof for snow, one egress window, under $14k” — and it designs it.
  3. Built to code. Engineered for Callicoon, NY (climate zone 6A): 50 psf snow, 48″ frost footings, R-21/49/30 insulation and egress. See the Code & Structure panel.
  4. Materials & cost. A live cut list with a running total and a link to order each item. Tap Permit-free to cap the footprint under 144 sq ft.
  5. See it your way. Switch between Shaded / Line / Color / Blueprint, step through an exploded Build-steps walkthrough, or open the dollhouse Interior.
  6. Take it with you. Save a render, print the plan sheet, or download a single 3D-print model — building, roof & model people in one file.
