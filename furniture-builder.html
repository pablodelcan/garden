Furniture Builder
Design a chair, table or bench you can actually build — and get the cut list.
  1. Pick the piece. Table, chair or bench — then tune size, leg style, and wood with the controls on the left. The 3D model updates live.
  2. Or just describe it. Tell the Architect what you want (“a walnut dining table, 6 ft, tapered legs”) and it lays it out.
  3. Cost it out — free. The running cost total and the build notes are always free. Unlock the full board-by-board cut list + a printable plan (with a buy-link for every part) once for $9; your design stays editable after.
  4. Check the build notes. Joinery (pocket screws / dowels), ergonomics, and a stability/weight read for your dimensions.
  5. See it your way. Shaded / Line / Color / Blueprint, spin it, and drop in a scale figure for proportion.
  6. Take it with you. Save a render, or download a single 3D-print model of the piece (with a scale figure beside it).
